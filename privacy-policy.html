Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us.

Last Updated: August 27, 2026

Part of the Plot ("we," "us," or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you provide when using our website or booking an immersive mystery game.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, how we protect it, and the choices you have regarding your information.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect information that you voluntarily provide to us when you contact us, request a booking, or participate in a Part of the Plot game.

This may include:

  • Your name
  • Email address
  • Phone number, if provided
  • Information about the event you are requesting or booking
  • Names and email addresses of guests or players when provided by the person organizing a game
  • Character assignments and information necessary to administer a game
  • Any other information you voluntarily include in a message or booking request

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect only for purposes reasonably necessary to operate Part of the Plot and provide our services.

This may include:

  • Responding to questions and inquiries
  • Communicating with you about a booking
  • Scheduling and administering games
  • Communicating important information about your event
  • Assigning characters to players
  • Sending character information and game materials
  • Providing guest guidelines and other necessary event information
  • Processing or confirming payments
  • Maintaining business and booking records
  • Preventing misuse of our services and protecting the security of our systems

3. Email Communications

If you provide an email address when requesting or booking a game, we may use it to communicate with you about that request or event.

For players participating in a game, we may send emails containing character assignments, character materials, guest guidelines, or other information necessary for participation.

These communications are considered service-related communications rather than general marketing messages.

4. Payment Information

Part of the Plot may use third-party payment services to process payments.

We do not intend to collect or store complete credit card numbers, debit card numbers, bank account numbers, or other sensitive payment credentials on our website.

Payment information may instead be handled directly by the applicable third-party payment provider, subject to that provider's own privacy policy and terms.

5. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services to help operate our website and business. These services may process information on our behalf.

Depending on the services currently in use, these may include providers for:

  • Website hosting
  • Database and application services
  • Email delivery
  • Contact forms
  • Payment processing

These providers may have access to information necessary to perform their services, but we do not authorize them to use your information for purposes unrelated to providing those services.

6. Cookies and Similar Technologies

Our website may use cookies or similar technologies when necessary for functionality, security, or third-party services.

We do not use cookies for the purpose of selling your personal information.

7. When We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information.

We may share information when reasonably necessary to:

  • Provide services you have requested
  • Communicate with you about your booking
  • Send game materials or character information
  • Process payments
  • Operate our website and business
  • Comply with applicable laws or legal requirements
  • Protect the rights, safety, property, or security of Part of the Plot or others

8. Player and Guest Information

If you organize a game, you may provide us with information about other guests who will participate in the event.

By providing another person's information, you should have permission to provide that information to us for the purpose of administering the event.

Player information may be used to assign characters and send players the information they need to participate in the game.

9. Character Materials

Character assignments and character materials may contain information intended only for the individual player receiving them.

Character materials may be delivered using temporary, private links. Players should not share private character materials with other players because doing so may reveal information that is intended to remain secret during the game.

10. How Long We Keep Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this Privacy Policy, including providing services, maintaining business records, resolving disputes, and complying with legal obligations.

Information that is no longer reasonably needed may be deleted or otherwise securely disposed of.

11. How We Protect Your Information

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect personal information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmitting or storing information electronically can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

12. Children's Privacy

Some Part of the Plot games may be appropriate for teenagers, depending on the specific game.

Our website is intended to be used by adults arranging or organizing events. We do not knowingly use our website to independently collect personal information directly from children.

Parents, guardians, or adult event organizers should provide any information necessary for minor participants.

13. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on where you live, you may have rights regarding your personal information, which may include the right to:

  • Request access to personal information we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of certain information
  • Ask questions about how your information is used

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the information provided below.

14. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, California law may provide you with additional rights regarding your personal information.

Part of the Plot does not sell personal information as that term is generally understood under applicable privacy laws.

California residents may contact us to ask about the personal information we collect and to request applicable privacy rights.

15. Links to Other Websites

Our website may contain links to third-party websites, including payment services or other resources.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices, content, or security of third-party websites. Please review the privacy policy of any third-party website you visit.

16. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time as our business, website, or legal requirements change.

When we make changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page.

17. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or would like to make a privacy-related request, please contact Part of the Plot through our Contact page .

Thank you for trusting Part of the Plot with your information.
