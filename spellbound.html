TREMAINE SCHOOL OF MAGIC

It's the final day of school.
Nothing will go as planned.

PLAYERS 13–17 Guests
At least 5 Female · 4 Male
LENGTH 3–3.5 Hours
AGES 12+
PARTICIPATION Highest Level
CONTENT Combat and Poison

THE STORY

Something has gone
terribly wrong.

The Headmaster has lost his memory, a mysterious warning has appeared, and the school is suddenly filled with questions.

With final exams approaching and tensions running high, students and professors must figure out who they can trust—and what is really happening.

Everyone has their own secrets, goals, and reasons for being at Tremaine.

YOUR DAY AT TREMAINE

There's more to do
than solve a mystery.

Step into your character's world and see where the story takes you.

I

Live Your Role

Step into the shoes of a student, professor, or other character and experience the day from their perspective.

II

Meet the Other Characters

Talk, negotiate, form alliances, make friends, and figure out who you can trust.

III

Follow the Mystery

Gather information and piece together what is happening at Tremaine.

IV

Keep Your Secrets

Your character has their own story. Decide what to share—and what to keep hidden.

V

Pursue Your Goals

Solving the mystery isn't necessarily the only thing your character wants.

VI

See Where the Story Leads

The game unfolds through the choices and interactions of the players.
