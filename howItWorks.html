What is an
immersive mystery?

In an immersive mystery, you become part of the story.

Instead of sitting back and watching characters solve a mystery, every player receives a role and becomes part of the world. You'll interact with the other characters, discover information, pursue your own goals, and decide what your character does next.

There's no audience. There's no stage. You're in the story.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING THE GAME?

Get Into Character

Every game is different, but the experience follows the same basic idea: you step into a character, interact with the other players, and let the story unfold.

01

Get Your Character

Before the game begins, you'll receive your character and the information you need to step into their role.

02

Meet the Cast

The other players aren't just your fellow guests—they're the other characters in the story.

03

Step Into the Story

Once the game begins, start interacting with the other characters and experience the story from your character's perspective.

04

Discover Information

Talk to people, ask questions, share what you know, and piece together the information you discover along the way.

05

Make Your Choices

Your character has their own goals, relationships, and secrets. Decide how you want to play your role and what you want to do.

06

See How It Unfolds

As everyone interacts and makes decisions, the mystery develops. By the end, you'll see how all the pieces fit together.
