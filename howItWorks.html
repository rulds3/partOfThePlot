WHAT IS

AN IMMERSIVE
MYSTERY?

In an immersive mystery, you become part of the story.

Instead of sitting back and watching characters solve a mystery, every player receives a role and becomes part of the world. You'll interact with the other characters, discover information, pursue your own goals, and decide what your character does next.

There's no audience. There's no stage. You're in the story.

WHAT HAPPENS DURING THE GAME?

Get Into Character

Every game is different, but the experience follows the same basic idea: you step into a character, interact with the other players, and let the story unfold.

01

Receive Your Character Assignment

Character notebooks and props will be distributed upon arrival at the party.

If your organizer chooses to provide them, you will also receive an electronic copy of your character notes ahead of time.

02

Get Into Character

Read through your character packet and get to know their story. What do they know? Who are their allies and enemies? What are their secrets and goals?

03

Step Into the Story

Once the game begins, start interacting with the other characters and experience the story from your character's perspective.

04

See How It Unfolds

Solve the central mystery and pursue your own goals as the tory unfolds around you. Every game is different!

05

Unravel the Mystery

At the end of the game, we’ll all come together to reveal the truth and see how everyone’s stories fit together.
