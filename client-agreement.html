PART OF THE PLOT

Client Agreement

Before You Book...

Please review the following terms before confirming your Part of the Plot experience.

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

1. Our Services

Part of the Plot provides immersive mystery parties and interactive role-playing entertainment experiences for private groups.

Depending on the game selected, the experience may include an interactive mystery game, character assignments and information, scripts, clues, documents, game materials, props, instructions, hosting, and facilitation by Part of the Plot.

Unless specifically agreed to in writing, Part of the Plot does not provide catering, alcoholic beverages, costumes, or general event decorations.

2. Organizer Responsibilities

The organizer is responsible for providing a suitable location for the event and ensuring that Part of the Plot has permission to conduct the event at that location.

The organizer is responsible for providing adequate space for the number of guests attending and for making sure the designated game area is reasonably safe and appropriate for normal participation.

Unless otherwise agreed, the organizer is also responsible for providing any tables, chairs, lighting, restroom access, food, beverages, and other general event necessities.

The organizer must inform Part of the Plot of any known hazards, restrictions, venue rules, or other conditions that could affect the event.

For additional guidance, please review our Organizer Responsibilities .

3. Guest Responsibilities

The organizer is responsible for communicating the event date, time, location, and relevant instructions to guests before the event.

Guests are expected to follow reasonable instructions from Part of the Plot hosts, treat other participants respectfully, use game materials appropriately, remain within designated areas, and follow applicable venue rules.

The organizer should make guests aware of the Guest Guidelines before the event.

4. Safety and Game Activities

Part of the Plot games are primarily social, theatrical, and interactive experiences. Some games may involve movement, searching for clues, handling props or materials, or moving around the designated game area.

Games are not intended to be athletic, fitness, amusement-park, or adventure activities unless a particular game description specifically states otherwise.

Participants are expected to use reasonable care and follow host instructions throughout the experience. Part of the Plot may modify, pause, or stop an activity when reasonably necessary for safety.

5. Alcohol, Drugs, and Impairment

Part of the Plot experiences are alcohol-free. Alcohol may not be consumed during the Part of the Plot experience.

Illegal drugs may not be used during an event. Participants who are impaired or whose behavior creates a safety concern may be asked to stop participating or leave the game.

6. Venue and Property

The organizer is responsible for obtaining any permission required to use the event location and for complying with applicable venue rules and restrictions.

The organizer and guests are responsible for damage they cause to the venue or its property, except to the extent the damage is caused by the negligence or intentional misconduct of Part of the Plot.

Participants must not enter restricted areas or use pools, trampolines, play equipment, or other potentially hazardous areas during the event unless specifically included as part of the game.

7. Accessibility and Accommodations

Part of the Plot will make reasonable efforts to accommodate accessibility needs when practical and when those accommodations can be provided safely.

The organizer should communicate known accessibility needs as early as possible so that reasonable arrangements can be considered before the event.

8. Reservations and Payment

A reservation request does not necessarily constitute a confirmed booking. A reservation becomes confirmed only after Part of the Plot has approved the reservation and all required confirmation and payment steps have been completed.

Pricing, deposit requirements, payment deadlines, and any applicable balance due will be communicated during the reservation process.

By submitting a reservation and agreeing to this Client Agreement, the organizer acknowledges responsibility for the payment obligations associated with the reservation.

9. Guest Count and Changes

The organizer is responsible for providing an accurate expected guest count when making a reservation.

Changes to the number of participants may affect game eligibility, character assignments, pricing, materials, staffing, or the ability to conduct the selected game.

Please notify Part of the Plot as soon as possible if the expected number of guests changes.

10. Cancellation and Rescheduling

Cancellations and requests to reschedule are subject to the cancellation and rescheduling terms provided by Part of the Plot at the time of booking.

Because Part of the Plot may purchase materials, reserve time, prepare game materials, and arrange staffing in advance, cancellation or rescheduling may affect amounts paid or amounts due.

The specific cancellation and rescheduling policy applicable to a reservation will be communicated as part of the booking process.

11. Cancellation by Part of the Plot

Part of the Plot may cancel or reschedule an event when circumstances make it unreasonable or unsafe to conduct the event as planned.

Such circumstances may include severe weather, illness or unavailability of a required host, significant venue problems, government restrictions, emergencies, or other circumstances outside the reasonable control of Part of the Plot.

When Part of the Plot cancels an event, the business will communicate available options regarding rescheduling or applicable refunds in accordance with the circumstances and the applicable booking terms.

12. Right to Pause or End an Event

Part of the Plot may pause, modify, or end an event when necessary because of unsafe conditions, inappropriate conduct, significant venue problems, failure to follow reasonable host instructions, or other circumstances that prevent the experience from being conducted safely or as agreed.

When reasonably possible, Part of the Plot will attempt to address the situation before ending the event.

Participants are responsible for their own conduct and for following reasonable safety instructions during the event.

Part of the Plot is not responsible for injuries, losses, or property damage resulting from the actions of participants, conditions outside the reasonable control of Part of the Plot, or the use of areas, equipment, or property not provided or controlled by Part of the Plot, except to the extent otherwise required by applicable law.

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to waive or limit any right or liability that cannot legally be waived or limited under applicable law.

To the extent permitted by applicable law, the organizer agrees to indemnify and hold harmless Part of the Plot and its owners, hosts, employees, and agents from claims, damages, losses, or expenses arising from the negligent or intentional acts of the organizer or guests, violations of this Agreement, unauthorized use of the venue, or property damage caused by the organizer or guests.

15. Photography and Recording

Participants may take photographs or recordings during an event provided that doing so does not interfere with the game, reveal confidential game information, or violate another person's privacy or applicable venue rules.

Part of the Plot may photograph or record portions of an event for business or promotional purposes only when appropriate permission or consent has been obtained.

17. Confidentiality and Spoilers

Participants are asked not to share solutions, character information, clues, plot details, or other information that could spoil the experience for future participants.

Organizers and guests agree to make a reasonable effort to preserve the mystery and enjoyment of the game for others.

18. Conduct

Part of the Plot is intended to be a fun and respectful experience for everyone.

Harassment, threats, violence, discrimination, deliberate property damage, dangerous behavior, or other seriously disruptive conduct is not permitted.

Part of the Plot reserves the right to remove a participant or end an event when reasonably necessary to protect the safety or well-being of participants, hosts, or property.
