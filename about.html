A Reason to Come Together.

I created Part of the Plot because I love watching people have fun.

I've always found that it's easier to connect with people when there's a reason to interact—a shared goal, something to figure out, or a story you're both a part of. Without that purpose, starting a conversation can sometimes feel surprisingly difficult.

What if the conversation was built into the activity?

That's where Part of the Plot began.

Instead of asking guests to come up with conversation topics or figure out how to break the ice, the game gives everyone a reason to interact. You have a character to play, a mystery to uncover, information to discover, and your own objectives to pursue.

The story gives you something to talk about.

And then something wonderful happens. People start talking, laughing, questioning each other, making connections, and getting caught up in the story. Before long, they're not worrying about what to say—they're simply having fun.

That's what I wanted Part of the Plot to create:

An environment where people can come together, interact naturally, and enjoy being part of something.

Because for me, the best part isn't just watching a mystery unfold.

It's watching people have fun together.
