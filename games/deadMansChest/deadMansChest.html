THE STORY

Welcome to Santo Oloroso.
Choose your allies carefully.

Santo Oloroso, 1784.

The Caribbean is a place of adventure, danger, and opportunity—and tonight, two pirate ships have arrived in the quiet waters of Santo Oloroso.

The Fury and the Scurvy Knave sit at anchor while the island's Taino inhabitants watch their unexpected guests with curiosity. The pirates claim to have business on the island, but exactly what brought them here is another matter.

Some have come seeking revenge for their fallen captain. Others may have their sights set on something far more valuable: a legendary treasure.

As the night unfolds, alliances will be tested, bargains will be struck, and loyalties may shift with the tide. Every pirate has their own motives, and trusting the wrong person could prove costly.
