THE STORY

Cactus Gulch
1884

For twenty years, this little frontier town has been home to prospectors, ranchers, gamblers, and anyone else looking for a fresh start—or a place where nobody asks too many questions.

Tonight, Cactus Gulch is celebrating its anniversary at the Silver Dollar Saloon. But the town is changing. The railroad is on its way, and not everyone agrees on what that means for the future.

Old disputes are boiling over. New faces have arrived in town. Deals are being made, cards are being played, and tensions are rising.

As the celebration gets underway, you'll step into the lives of the people who call Cactus Gulch home—and discover that everyone has their own reasons for being there.
