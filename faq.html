General Questions

Each Part of the Plot mystery includes a 3.5 hour hosted experience with character booklets, personal goals, secrets, and themed props. Your host guides you as you interact, uncover secrets, and become part of the story. Public tickets also include a light meal.

  • Premium: Includes additional themed props and requires two hosts to run the experience.
  • Classic: Our traditional mystery party experience, with everything your group needs to become part of the story.
  • Quick: Shorter experiences designed for groups who want an interactive mystery without committing to a full-length game. (In development.)

Part of the Plot is perfect for anyone looking for something more interactive than a traditional party or dinner. Our mysteries are great for friends, families, birthdays, holiday gatherings, community groups, apartment community events, and other social occasions.

Our games are suitable for ages 12+, though public events may target specific age groups. Check the event registration details for age recommendations and themes.

Private parties can accommodate teens, adults, or mixed-age groups. Players should be able to read and understand character materials independently, though younger players may participate with help from an adult or older player.

Private Games

You provide the space and the cast! Your venue should have approximately 800 square feet or more, with room for guests to mingle and have private conversations. Your home, backyard, apartment community room, clubhouse, or a rented event space can all work well. Food and decorations are optional. See “What if I choose to provide food?” below for tips.

Our games are designed to keep the action moving! We recommend a buffet-style meal or finger foods that guests can enjoy while mingling and interacting with one another. A formal sit-down meal isn't recommended.

No problem! Your group size can vary within the game's recommended range. How you assign characters can affect how easily we can accommodate changes:

  • Characters assigned ahead of time: Players can prepare for their roles before the game. If an essential character is unable to attend, we may need to make a few adjustments to the cast, but we'll help make it work.
  • Characters assigned at the party: Players won't have as much time to prepare beforehand, but changes to the group are generally easier to accommodate. As long as the minimum number of players attends, there shouldn't be a problem.
