Wondering what to expect?
We've got some answers.

Your game includes everything you need to play, including the story, character information, clues, instruction, materials needed for the game, and a host to run it! Just gather your group and get ready to to become part of the plot!

Our games come in three different types, so you can choose the experience that best fits your group and occasion.

  • Premium: Our most immersive experiences, designed for a full evening of interactive mystery and storytelling.
  • Classic: Our traditional mystery party experience, with everything your group needs to become part of the story.
  • Quick: Shorter experiences designed for groups who want an interactive mystery without committing to a full-length game. (In the works.)

You provide the space and the cast. Depending on the game, you may also want to provide food, drinks, costumes, or decorations to make the experience even more fun - but these are optional. We provide the game and everything you need to play!

Our games take place wherever you choose to host them! You'll need a space large enough for your group to mingle and have private converations when the plot calls for it. A home, community room, event space, or similar location can work well.

We currently travel to locations within approximately 30 minutes of Idaho Falls. If you are outside that area, feel free to reach out-we may still be able to accommodate you!

Our games are designed to keep the action moving! We recommend a buffet-style meal or finger foods that guests can enjoy while mingling and interacting with one another. A formal sit-down meal isn't recommended.

Part of the Plot games are designed for groups who want to do something more interactive than a traditional party or dinner. They're great for friends, families, birthday celebrations, holiday gatherings, community groups, and other social events. Some games may also work well for workplace or team-building events, depending on the group and the game.

Each game has its own recommended age range, so be sure to check the individual game description before purchasing. Our games involve reading character information and other game materials, so players should be comfortable reading and understanding the material independently. Younger players can participate with help from an adult or older player when appropriate.

No problem! Your group size can vary within the game's recommended range. How you assign characters can affect how easily we can accommodate changes:

  • Characters assigned ahead of time: Players can prepare for their roles before the game. If an essential character is unable to attend, we may need to make a few adjustments to the cast, but we'll help make it work.
  • Characters assigned at the party: Players won't have as much time to prepare beforehand, but changes to the group are generally easier to accommodate. As long as the minimum number of players attends, there shouldn't be a problem.

Here are the details you need to know when booking your Part of the Plot experience.

  • Payment: We accept cash and major credit and debit cards through Stripe. Venmo is also available upon request. A deposit is required to reserve your game date.
  • Deposit: A deposit of 50% is required to reserve your date. The remaining balance is due 7 days before your game.
  • Refunds:
    • More than 14 days before: Deposit is fully refundable or transferable.
    • 7–14 days before: Deposit is transferable to a new date, subject to availability.
    • Less than 7 days before: Deposit is nonrefundable.
