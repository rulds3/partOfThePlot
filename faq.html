Wondering what to expect?
We've got some answers.

Your game includes everything you need to play, including the story, character information, clues, instruction, materials needed for the game, and a host to run it! Just gather your group and get ready to to become part of the plot!

Food, costumes, decorations, and a venue are not included. Feel free to add your own touches and make the game your own!

You provide the space and the cast. Depending on the game, you may also want to provide food, drinks, costumes, or decorations to make the experience even more fun - but these are optional. We provide the game and everything you need to play!

Our games are designed to keep the action moving! We recommend a buffet-style meal or finger foods that guests can enjoy while mingling and interacting with one another. A formal sit-down meal isn't recommended.

Absolutely not! The games are designed so that anyone can participate. You don't have to be an actor to become part of the story.

Our games take place wherever you choose to host them! You'll need a space large enough for your group to mingle and have private converations when the plot calls for it. A home, community room, event space, or similar location can work well.

We currently travel to locations within approximately 30 minutes of Idaho Falls. If you are outside that area, feel free to reach out-we may still be able to accommodate you!

Absolutely! Our games are available to purchase, so you can host your own game with your friends, family, or group. Some of our games are created by independent game designers, while others will be original games written by Part of the Plot.

Each game has its own recommended age range, so be sure to check the individual game description before purchasing. Our games involve reading character information and other game materials, so players should be comfortable reading and understanding the material independently. Younger players can participate with help from an adult or older player when appropriate.
